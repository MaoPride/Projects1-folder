What is your favorite anime?

Attack on Titan is another popular anime. It features a dark and unsettling atmosphere. The animation is impressive and the plot is engaging.

Demon Slayer is a popular anime. It has a unique art style and an engaging story. The characters are well-developed and the animation is stunning.
